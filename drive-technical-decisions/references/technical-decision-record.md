## Session checks

- Is there exactly one final decision right?
- Can participants challenge criteria and assumptions rather than only the final ranking?
- Were implementers and operators heard before closure?
- Does the decision still make sense if the preferred implementation is delayed?
- Is reopening based on evidence or changed context rather than disappointment?
