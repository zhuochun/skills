## Attempt review

1. What outcome and signals did the learner expect?
2. Which options and tradeoffs did they consider?
3. What happened, including luck or environmental effects?
4. Which part of the model changed?
5. What should be repeated, isolated, or made harder next?

## Guardrail check

- Is this development rather than evaluation?
- Does the learner have meaningful agency?
- Is failure safe enough to teach rather than harm?
- Are criteria equally available and relevant to the role?
- Would the assignment still be worthwhile if no promotion followed?
