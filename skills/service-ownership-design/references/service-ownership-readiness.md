## Enabling conditions

Rate as demonstrated, partial, absent, or not applicable and cite evidence.

- Fast trusted tests and deployment controls
- Customer-proximate health signals and diagnostics
- Runbooks, metadata, routing, and escalation
- Self-service infrastructure and safe defaults
- Production access and decision authority
- Incident support and specialist pathways
- Capacity, dependency, cost, and compliance context
- Learning and improvement capacity after incidents
