## Session checks

- Were discoverable facts inspected before questions were asked?
- Were low-sensitivity independent questions batched, and disclosure-dependent or gating questions serialized?
- Did the owner supply goals, values, authority, and risk tolerance before a firm recommendation?
- Were criteria and assumptions challengeable rather than only the final ranking?
- Does the disposition match the current evidence and authority?
