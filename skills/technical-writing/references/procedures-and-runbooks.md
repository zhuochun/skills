# Procedures and Runbooks

Use a procedure or runbook when a reader must perform recurring or
failure-sensitive work safely.

- State preconditions, required authority, signals, tools, and hazards before
  the actions they control.
- Preserve critical order, checkpoints, expected observations, and stop or
  escalation conditions.
- Separate routine deterministic actions from judgment that belongs to an
  accountable operator.
- Include recovery, rollback, or safe-state guidance when action can worsen the
  situation.
- Keep the artifact reachable and revisable in the workflow where it is used.
- Validate through rehearsal, real use, or another proportionate execution
  check.

Complete the procedure when an authorized reader can perform or safely stop the
task without relying on undocumented expert memory.
